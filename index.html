01 AI development & consulting

Practical AI.
Connected to your work.

We develop with Claude and ChatGPT and consult on how to apply them to real business workflows. Our experience spans quotation development, engineering knowledge and meeting prototypes.

01 / DOCUMENTS

RFQ & specification analysis

Extract requirements from requests for quotation (RFQs), specifications and technical documents. Compare revisions, flag gaps and prepare clarification lists for a reviewer.

Useful for Turning an incoming RFQ package into a review packet with source references.

Claude / ChatGPT · Document extraction

02 / QUOTATIONS

Quotation workflows

Connect equipment data, bills of materials and cost inputs to reviewable quotation drafts. Keep quantities, assumptions and approval steps visible throughout the workflow.

Useful for Preparing a draft workbook that an estimator can check before any quote is issued.

Python · Excel / JSON · Validation rules

03 / KNOWLEDGE

Ontology & knowledge graphs

Define a shared model of equipment, materials, requirements and documents. Use Neo4j knowledge graphs to connect those concepts with their relationships and evidence.

Useful for Tracing which requirement affects a component, calculation or quotation decision.

Ontology · Neo4j · OWL / SHACL

04 / RETRIEVAL & TOOLS

Answers connected to sources

Develop retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over agreed document sets. Connect agents to business tools through APIs and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), with scoped access and review points.

Useful for A document assistant that returns source locations and drafts the next action for review.

RAG · Agents · MCP · API integration

05 / ENGINEERING

Engineering calculation support

Combine AI-assisted input preparation with deterministic calculation code. Record units, assumptions and calculation references so an engineer can inspect the result.

Useful for Building repeatable calculation workflows with checks and human engineering review.

Python · Calculation checks · Traceable inputs

06 / MEETING PROTOTYPES

Transcription & translation

Prototype meeting transcription, translated captions and contextual reply suggestions. Evaluate technical vocabulary and language quality using agreed sample recordings.

Useful for Testing English–Korean support for technical discussions before wider use.

Speech-to-text · Translation · Desktop prototypes

Start with a scoped pilot.

These capabilities reflect hands-on development and prototype experience. Consulting can define the workflow, build a pilot and evaluate it with your team. Production use requires a separate review of performance, data access, operation and support. Quotations and engineering decisions remain subject to human approval.

04 Working with us

Good support begins
with the right details.

Have a part to source or an AI workflow to explore? Start with a clear task, the available information and the person who will review the result.

  1. 01↗

    Define the requirement.

    Describe the equipment or business bottleneck. For AI work, choose one workflow, name its reviewer and agree what a useful result should look like.

  2. 02↗

    Review the inputs.

    Share part numbers, quantities or drawings for a plant inquiry. For AI consulting, map the documents, systems and access limits using a small, agreed sample.

  3. 03↗

    Test, review, then decide.

    Agree the supply or support scope. For AI development, test a pilot against sample cases, review its errors and source evidence, then define any rollout and handover.

↳ A nameplate photo, part drawing or short description of a repetitive task is a useful place to start.
