Changelog

What we shipped, newest first.

October 2026

Stampwise: pilots open, docs and pricing published

  • The quotation agent is now called Stampwise.
  • Pilot deployments opened for other pressure vessel fabricators and plant suppliers.
  • Published product docs, pricing, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  • Rebuilt the website around the quotation agent.

September 2026

Deterministic ASME engine connected

  • ASME Section VIII Division 1 and 2 calculation engine connected to the agent through MCP.
  • Thickness and allowable stress checks run in code with source approved tables, outside the language model.

Early 2026

In daily use on live bids

  • The quotation agent became part of the daily bid process at an ASME U stamp pressure vessel fabricator in Korea.
  • Review gates added so that no price leaves without a named estimator signing off.

2025

First Claude quotation pipeline

  • Built with Claude Code: RFQ extraction, requirement lists with sources, quotation workbook drafting and deterministic pricing gates.
  • Neo4j ontology linking requirements, components, calculations and prices.

April 2025

Seoul Plant Service founded

  • Established in Dangjin, Republic of Korea, to build AI products for the industrial plant supply chain.
